# Product Guide: CarbonOpus Ecosystem

## Vision

CarbonOpus is a decentralized ecosystem that empowers creators by providing a fair, transparent, and feature-rich platform for launching new cryptocurrencies and distributing music as NFTs. It combines the excitement of token creation with an artist-centric marketplace, aiming to become the go-to platform for both emerging crypto projects and digital musicians.

## Target Audience

The platform is designed to serve a diverse range of users within the Web3 ecosystem:

*   **Token Creators:** Individuals or teams who want to launch their own cryptocurrency (`CarbonCoin`) with a fair and bot-resistant mechanism, using a bonding curve for initial price discovery.
*   **Artists & Musicians:** Creators who wish to tokenize their music as NFTs, enabling direct sales to their audience while benefiting from automated royalty and referral distributions.
*   **Traders & Collectors:** Users interested in discovering and investing in new, emerging tokens and collecting unique music NFTs from their favorite artists.
*   **Fans & Music Resellers:** Supporters of artists who not only purchase music for their collection but may also participate in a secondary market, benefiting from the platform's transparent royalty structures.

## Core Features

The CarbonOpus platform is built on a foundation of several key features:

*   **Token Launchpad (`CarbonCoinLauncher`):** A factory contract that allows any user to easily deploy a new `CarbonCoin` token. Each token is equipped with a bonding curve for its initial trading phase, ensuring fair price discovery.
*   **Music NFT Marketplace (`CarbonOpus`):** An ERC-1155 based marketplace where artists can mint and sell their music as NFTs. The system is designed to be artist-first, with features that support their creative work.
*   **Automated Royalty & Referral System:** A built-in mechanism that automatically distributes a portion of every music NFT sale to the original artist and to any user who referred the buyer. This creates a powerful incentive structure that rewards both creators and their supporters.

## Key Differentiator

While the platform offers both token launching and NFT marketplace functionalities, its primary differentiator is its **Artist-Centric Model**. Unlike generic NFT platforms, CarbonOpus is purpose-built for musicians. The integrated royalty and referral system ensures that artists are fairly compensated for their work, not just on the initial sale but also on secondary sales, fostering a sustainable creative economy. This focus makes it a uniquely attractive platform for artists looking to engage with their audience in the Web3 space.
